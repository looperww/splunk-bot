# Business Email Compromise

Use when the investigation concerns suspected mailbox compromise or phishing-enabled account abuse. Correlate mailbox activity, authentication anomalies, forwarding/delegation changes, and related users or messages.

Splunk Bot should keep evidence collection read-only and scoped to the approved incident.
